THE ACTUAL STUFF

WHAT I BUILT

The farming loop is tied into the adventure instead of sitting off to the side. Talk to characters to get carrot seeds, find a plot, and plant them to move the story forward.

Some planting spots do more than grow crops: a special plot kicks off a boss sequence, connecting exploration and farming with the game's combat encounters.

GAMEPLAY
