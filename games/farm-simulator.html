THE ACTUAL STUFF

WHAT I BUILT

Farm Simulator deliberately opens like a gentle farming RPG. Step out from the farmhouse, explore the crop fields, talk to characters, collect carrot seeds and plant them. Planting at a special plot triggers the carrot boss reveal and fight—the first big break from the cozy farming premise.

Encounters at the village market and with the angel, soldiers and knights keep escalating the story until it becomes a distinct top-down combat finale. Move, aim attacks, dash past enemies and survive successive waves. The contrast between the quiet farm opening and the sudden fights is the point; this isn't a crop-economy simulator.
