THE
MECHANICS

  • Four player controls: move and turn, jump, punch, and dodge.
  • Punches chain through three animation-triggered combo stages.
  • Enemy AI approaches, then selects from animation-triggered attacks, including jump punches, dash attacks, and head hits.
  • Health bars track both fighters; the round ends when a fighter reaches zero health.
