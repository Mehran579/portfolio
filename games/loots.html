THE ACTUAL STUFF

WHAT I BUILT

Every slash and dash leaves a trail behind in the arena. When a new trail crosses an existing one and closes a loop, the enclosed area becomes a damaging circuit that hits enemies caught inside.

Clear successive enemy waves, keep moving through the arena, and choose an upgrade between rounds. The main challenge is turning your movement and attacks into a useful circuit while enemies close in.

THE
MECHANICS

  • Slash toward your aim to draw a curved energy trail.
  • Dash across the arena to leave a straight trail behind.
  • Connect trails to close a circuit and damage enemies inside it.
  • Survive escalating waves with chasers and ranged attackers.
  • Pick an upgrade between waves to adapt your run.
