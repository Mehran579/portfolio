Drone Sim

A physics-driven drone, a missile launcher, and soldiers who shoot back.
A 3D Unity prototype built for the iPage UM Services Unity Skill Assessment. Pilot a drone through a test arena, engage patrolling enemy soldiers, and use missile explosions to send nearby physics objects flying.

THE ACTUAL STUFF

WHAT I BUILT

The drone uses Rigidbody forces for flight. WASD movement is relative to the camera, Q and E control altitude, and procedural pitch and roll react to movement input. The follow camera keeps the drone in view as it turns and moves through the arena.

Fire missiles with a cooldown. Each launch applies recoil to the drone; on impact, the missile creates an explosion that pushes nearby physics objects. NavMesh-driven soldiers patrol waypoints, detect the player with range and line-of-sight checks, and fire when the drone comes into range.

THE
CONTROLS

  • WASD: camera-relative horizontal flight.
  • Q / E: ascend and descend.
  • Mouse: look around with the follow camera.
  • Space: fire a missile, subject to its cooldown.
  • Restart: reload the current scene.
