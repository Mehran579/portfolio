04 / the devlog entry

THE BORING-SOUNDING STUFF

Dear whoever is reading this:

I'm a self-taught programmer and a first-year IT student at ADGIPS, Delhi. I don't have professional studio experience yet. What I do have is a suspiciously large pile of finished little games and a habit of opening Unity to "fix one thing" and emerging four hours later with a wave spawner.

I care more about making the mechanic work than pretending I made the art. A lot of the visual assets are free/asset-store stuff, Mixamo animations, or art from teammates. That's fine. My lane is the machinery underneath the joke.
